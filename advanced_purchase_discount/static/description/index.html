Advanced Purchase & Vendor Bill Discounts

Apply a percentage or a fixed amount to a purchase order or a vendor bill and let Odoo spread it proportionally, by your own weights, as one discount line per tax group, or from volume tiers. The result is exact to the cent and flows from the order to the receipt valuation, the bill and the journal entries.

Why this module

Vendors grant discounts as lump sums, percentages on the whole order or volume deals. Odoo 16 has no tool to apply them to a purchase order.

In standard Odoo 16

  • Purchase order lines have no discount field: buyers lower the unit price by hand.
  • A fixed amount (e.g. “$ 250 off”) must be split across lines manually, and the cents rarely add up.
  • There is no order-level discount and no discount line split by tax rate.
  • Vendor pricelists give price breaks by quantity, but no agreement discount and no rules by category, amount or whole order.
  • The vendor bill shows the reduced price with no link to the negotiated discount.
  • Nobody can tell who changed the prices, when and why.

With this module

  • A real Disc. (%) column on purchase order lines; the unit price stays the vendor price.
  • Fixed amounts are allocated exactly and checked against Odoo’s tax engine: $ 250 off is $ 250.00.
  • A Discount button with four modes, including one negative line per tax group.
  • Agreement Discount (%) on vendor pricelists plus Volume Discount Tiers by quantity, amount, vendor, product, category or whole document.
  • The discount travels with the line: receipt valuation, vendor bill and journal entries use the net cost.
  • A Discount Log records every operation, line by line.

How it works

Four steps, from configuration to the posted bill.

1
Enable
Turn on Advanced Purchase Discounts for the company and pick the discount service product.
2
Open the wizard
Click Discount on an RFQ, a purchase order or a draft vendor bill.
3
Preview
Choose a mode and a value; the new discount and subtotal of every line appear before anything is saved.
4
Apply
The document, receipt, bill and accounting follow; the operation is written to the Discount Log.

Features

Screenshots use the Odoo 16 demo data (vendors Wood Corner, Azure Interior and Ready Mat) in USD, with 15% and 5% purchase taxes and average-cost, real-time inventory valuation.

Menus

Everything sits in the Purchase app

Purchase › Reporting › Discount Log lists every discount applied. Purchase › Configuration › Volume Discount Tiers holds the tier rules. The Discount button and a Discounts smart button appear on purchase orders and vendor bills.

Feature 1

One switch per company, with a validated discount product

Purchase › Configuration › Settings › Invoicing › Advanced Purchase Discounts is off by default. When you turn it on, a Discount Product is required. It is checked on save: it must be a purchasable service, belong to the company (or be shared), have an expense account for that company that is not deprecated, and carry purchase taxes only. A ready-made Purchase Discount product is created on install. Apply Vendor Agreement Discounts copies the vendor’s agreement discount onto new order lines.

Feature 2

Mode A: spread a discount proportionally

Enter a Percentage to apply it to every selected line, or a Fixed Amount to spread it Proportional to line amount, Proportional to quantity or Evenly on each line. Untick Use to leave a line out. The Preview shows the untaxed amount before and after, and each line’s New Disc. (%) and New Subtotal. Here, $ 500 off a $ 9,300.00 order gives exactly $ 8,800.00.

Feature 3

Mode B: weight the strategic lines

With Total discount split by weights, a weight of 3 takes three times the share of a weight of 1; a weight of 0 leaves the line untouched. Below, $ 600 is split 3 : 2 : 1 into $ 300, $ 200 and $ 100. You can also enter a Specific percentage per line or a Specific amount per line.

Specific percentage per line: 5%, 12.5%, none and 8%.
Feature 4

Mode C: one discount line per tax group

Instead of touching the lines, the wizard adds a negative line with the discount product for each combination of taxes on the document. The amount is split according to each group’s untaxed total, so the tax is reduced correctly at each rate. Tax-included prices and fiscal-position taxes are kept. On purchase orders the discount lines are shown in blue and locked against manual edits.

8% on an order mixing 15% and 5% taxes: −$ 733.50 at 15% and −$ 71.92 at 5%.
Feature 5

Mode D: smart tiered volume discounts

No value to type: the wizard evaluates the vendor’s agreement discount and every Volume Discount Tier that matches the vendor, the date, the product or category, and the quantity or untaxed amount. The best line discount wins, then the Whole Document tier is applied on top. The preview explains which rule applied to which line. On this Wood Corner order: 10% on 120 chairs, 5% on desks above $ 10,000, 6% on 60 drawers, then the 2% contract tier above $ 25,000.

Feature 6

Tier rules and vendor agreements

A tier has a Scope (Per Line or Whole Document), a threshold on Quantity or Untaxed Amount (in company currency), a Discount (%), optional Vendors, Products, Product Categories (child categories included) and validity dates. A Whole Document tier is applied Prorate on Lines or as a Dedicated Discount Line. On vendor pricelists, the Agreement Discount (%) column records the contract discount for each price break.

Feature 7

A clear purchase order and printout

Order lines show Disc.% next to the vendor’s Unit Price; the totals add Gross Amount and Total Discount above the untaxed amount. The printed purchase order gets a Disc.% column and the same summary, shown only when the order has a discount.

Feature 8

Receipts valued at the discounted cost

The stock move price is the net price, so inventory valuation reflects what you really pay: the Office Chair at $ 129.45 with a 5.576799% discount is valued at $ 122.23. The optional PO Disc. (%) column on receipts shows the discount of the originating line. Changing a discount on a confirmed order updates the price of the receipts not yet done.

Feature 9

Bills that match the order, entries that balance

Bills created from the order carry the same Disc.%; the optional PO Disc. (%) column shows the order value side by side. Because receipt and bill use the same net cost, the stock interim account is fully reconciled and no price-difference entry is created. Gross $ 4,482.95, discount $ 250.00, untaxed $ 4,232.95.

Feature 10

Warning when a bill drifts from the order

If a draft bill line discount differs from its purchase order line, the line turns orange and a banner appears. One click on Use the purchase order discounts realigns the bill.

Feature 11

Discounts directly on vendor bills

The same wizard works on draft vendor bills and refunds, with or without a purchase order. Here, a $ 300 early-settlement discount is added as two lines (15% and 5% taxes), posted to the discount product’s expense account Purchase Discounts Received.

Feature 12

A complete audit trail

Each application creates a PDISC/YYYY/NNNNN record with the document, vendor, mode, entered value, Untaxed Before / Untaxed After, Discount Applied, Rounding Residual, user and Reason, plus one detail line per changed line. A message is also posted in the document chatter. Filter by Purchase Orders, Vendor Bills or With Rounding Residual; group by mode, vendor or date.

Feature 13

Fully translated into Arabic

All 218 terms are translated: settings, wizard, tiers, log, reports, error messages and the discount product. Odoo’s right-to-left layout is supported.

The four modes at a glance

Pick the mode that matches how the vendor granted the discount.

A · Proportional same % on every line, or a fixed amount by line amount, quantity or evenly
B · Weighted your weights, or a specific % or amount per line
C · Discount line one negative line per tax group, posted to a discount account
D · Tiered vendor agreements and volume tiers, evaluated automatically
Exact to the cent: a fixed amount is first allocated in whole cents (largest remainder, never more than a line’s amount). Each line’s percentage is then solved and checked against Odoo’s own subtotal calculation, using the Purchase Discount precision (6 decimals). Example: $ 9.99 off 3 × $ 33.33 = $ 99.99 gives a discount of 9.990999% and an untaxed amount of exactly $ 90.00. Replace Existing Discounts (default) starts from the gross prices; untick it to stack, e.g. 10% then 10% = 19%.

Built for real-world corner cases

What happens in the situations that usually break discount tools.

Tax-included prices
Fixed amounts are tax excluded; price-included taxes are handled in all modes, including the price of Mode C lines.
Fiscal positions
Discount lines use the taxes already mapped on the lines; the expense account is mapped by the fiscal position.
Discount above the amount
Blocked with a clear message; no line can go below zero, and the preview shows the warning before you apply.
Already billed lines
Excluded from the wizard; billed discount lines cannot be removed. Posted bills and locked or cancelled orders are refused.
Confirmed orders
Discounts can still change before billing; pending receipts get the new net price, and removed discount lines are set to quantity 0.
Refunds
Vendor refunds accept every mode, and the tax engine runs in refund mode.
Units of measure and currencies
Quantity tiers use the product unit of measure; amount tiers convert the document currency into the company currency at the document date.
Multi-company
Setting, discount product, tiers and log are per company, with record rules. A company without the setting cannot open the wizard.
Dedicated discount line billing
A Mode C line on a purchase order is billed in full on the first bill, whatever the product’s billing policy.

Security

Uses the standard Purchase and Invoicing groups; no new group to manage.
