Advanced Purchase & Vendor Bill Discounts

Apply a percentage or a fixed amount to a purchase order or a vendor bill and let Odoo spread it proportionally, by your own weights, as one discount line per tax group, or from volume tiers. The result is exact to the cent and flows from the order to the receipt valuation, the bill and the journal entries.

Why this module

Vendors grant discounts as lump sums, percentages on the whole order or volume deals. Odoo 19 has a discount per line, but no tool to apply an order-level or fixed-amount discount.

In standard Odoo 19

  • The Disc.% column works line by line, with 2 decimals: there is no order-level discount.
  • A fixed amount (e.g. “$ 250 off”) must be turned into line percentages by hand, and the cents rarely add up.
  • There is no discount line split by tax rate.
  • Vendor pricelists carry a discount per price break, but no rules by category, amount or whole order.
  • Changing a quantity resets the line discount from the vendor pricelist, and confirming an order copies one-off discounts into the vendor pricelist.
  • Nobody can tell who changed the discounts, when and why.

With this module

  • A Discount button applies a percentage or a fixed amount to the whole order or bill.
  • Fixed amounts are allocated exactly and checked against Odoo’s tax engine: $ 250 off is $ 250.00.
  • Four modes, including one negative line per tax group.
  • Volume Discount Tiers by quantity, amount, vendor, product, category or whole document, combined with the pricelist discount.
  • Wizard discounts survive quantity changes and are not copied into vendor pricelists; receipt valuation, bill and journal entries use the net cost.
  • A Discount Log records every operation, line by line.

How it works

Four steps, from configuration to the posted bill.

1
Enable
Turn on Advanced Purchase Discounts for the company and pick the discount service product.
2
Open the wizard
Click Discount on an RFQ, a purchase order or a draft vendor bill.
3
Preview
Choose a mode and a value; the new discount and subtotal of every line appear before anything is saved.
4
Apply
The document, receipt, bill and accounting follow; the operation is written to the Discount Log.

Features

Screenshots use the Odoo 19 demo data (vendors Wood Corner, Azure Interior and Ready Mat) in USD, with 15% and 5% purchase taxes and average-cost, real-time inventory valuation.

Menus

Everything sits in the Purchase app

Purchase › Reporting › Discount Log lists every discount applied. Purchase › Configuration › Volume Discount Tiers holds the tier rules. The Discount button and a Discounts smart button appear on purchase orders and vendor bills.

Feature 1

One switch per company, with a validated discount product

Purchase › Configuration › Settings › Invoicing › Advanced Purchase Discounts is off by default. When you turn it on, a Discount Product is required. It is checked on save: it must be a purchasable service, belong to the company (or be shared), have an expense account for that company that is not deprecated, and carry purchase taxes only. A ready-made Purchase Discount product is created on install. The Volume Discount Tiers link opens the tier rules.

Feature 2

Mode A: spread a discount proportionally

Enter a Percentage to apply it to every selected line, or a Fixed Amount to spread it Proportional to line amount, Proportional to quantity or Evenly on each line. Untick Use to leave a line out. The Preview shows the untaxed amount before and after, and each line’s New Disc. (%) and New Subtotal. Here, $ 500 off a $ 9,300.00 order gives exactly $ 8,800.00.

Feature 3

Mode B: weight the strategic lines

With Total discount split by weights, a weight of 3 takes three times the share of a weight of 1; a weight of 0 leaves the line untouched. Below, $ 600 is split 3 : 2 : 1 into $ 300, $ 200 and $ 100. You can also enter a Specific percentage per line or a Specific amount per line.

Specific percentage per line: 5%, 12.5%, none and 8%.
Feature 4

Mode C: one discount line per tax group

Instead of touching the lines, the wizard adds a negative line with the discount product for each combination of taxes on the document. The amount is split according to each group’s untaxed total, so the tax is reduced correctly at each rate. Tax-included prices and fiscal-position taxes are kept. On purchase orders the discount lines are shown in blue, locked against manual edits, and billed in full on the first bill.

8% on an order mixing 15% and 5% taxes: −$ 733.50 at 15% and −$ 71.92 at 5%.
Feature 5

Mode D: smart tiered volume discounts

No value to type: the wizard evaluates the vendor pricelist discount and every Volume Discount Tier that matches the vendor, the date, the product or category, and the quantity or untaxed amount. The best line discount wins, then the Whole Document tier is applied on top. The preview explains which rule applied to which line. On this Wood Corner order: 10% on 120 chairs, 5% on desks above $ 10,000, 6% on 60 drawers, then the 2% contract tier above $ 25,000.

Feature 6

Tier rules and vendor pricelist discounts

A tier has a Scope (Per Line or Whole Document), a threshold on Quantity or Untaxed Amount (in company currency), a Discount (%), optional Vendors, Products, Product Categories (child categories included) and validity dates. A Whole Document tier is applied Prorate on Lines or as a Dedicated Discount Line. Vendor agreements use Odoo’s native Discount (%) on vendor pricelists (4% and 7.5% on the Wood Corner chair below). When an order is confirmed, Odoo 19 adds the vendor to the pricelist and copies the line discount; the module keeps the vendor price but does not copy a discount set by the wizard (Azure Interior below: $ 129.45 at 0%).

Feature 7

A clear purchase order and printout

The Disc.% column, hidden by default in Odoo 19, is shown on order lines while the Unit Price stays the vendor price; the totals add Gross Amount and Total Discount above the untaxed amount. On the printed purchase order, the Disc. column prints without trailing zeros (11.8 rather than 11.800000), and the same summary is added when the order has a discount.

Feature 8

Receipts valued at the discounted cost

Receipts are valued at the net price, so inventory valuation reflects what you really pay: the 12 Office Chair Black at $ 129.45 with a 5.576799% discount are valued at $ 1,466.77 ($ 122.23 each). Odoo 19 stores this value on the stock move; Inventory › Reporting › Moves Analysis shows it in the Value column. The optional PO Disc. (%) column on receipts shows the discount of the originating line. If you change a discount on a confirmed order, receipts validated afterwards use the new net price.

Feature 9

Bills that match the order, entries that balance

Bills created from the order carry the same Disc.%; the optional PO Disc. (%) column shows the order value side by side. In Odoo 19 the bill posts the goods on the Stock Valuation account at the same net cost as the receipt moves, so no price-difference line is created. Gross $ 4,482.95, discount $ 250.00, untaxed $ 4,232.95.

Feature 10

Warning when a bill drifts from the order

If a draft bill line discount differs from its purchase order line, the line turns orange and a banner appears. One click on Use the purchase order discounts realigns the bill.

Feature 11

Discounts directly on vendor bills

The same wizard works on draft vendor bills and refunds (the tax engine runs in refund mode), with or without a purchase order. Here, a $ 300 early-settlement discount is added as two lines (15% and 5% taxes), posted to the discount product’s expense account Purchase Discounts Received.

Feature 12

A complete audit trail

Each application creates a PDISC/YYYY/NNNNN record with the document, vendor, mode, entered value, Untaxed Before / Untaxed After, Discount Applied, Rounding Residual, user and Reason, plus one detail line per changed line. A message is also posted in the document chatter. Filter by Purchase Orders, Vendor Bills or With Rounding Residual; group by mode, vendor or date.

Feature 13

Fully translated into Arabic

All 209 terms are translated: settings, wizard, tiers, log, reports, error messages and the discount product. Odoo’s right-to-left layout is supported.

The four modes at a glance

Pick the mode that matches how the vendor granted the discount.

A · Proportional same % on every line, or a fixed amount by line amount, quantity or evenly
B · Weighted your weights, or a specific % or amount per line
C · Discount line one negative line per tax group, posted to a discount account
D · Tiered vendor pricelist discounts and volume tiers, evaluated automatically
Exact to the cent: a fixed amount is first allocated in whole cents (largest remainder, never more than a line’s amount). Each line’s percentage is then solved and checked against Odoo’s own subtotal calculation, using the Purchase Discount precision (6 decimals). Since Odoo 18, documents are rounded from raw line amounts and, with Round Globally, per tax group, so the engine solves each line on its raw amount and the document total still lands on the cent. Example: $ 9.99 off 3 × $ 33.33 = $ 99.99 gives a discount of 9.990999% and an untaxed amount of exactly $ 90.00. Replace Existing Discounts (default) starts from the gross prices; untick it to stack, e.g. 10% then 10% = 19%.

Built for real-world corner cases

What happens in the situations that usually break discount tools.

Tax-included prices and rounding
Fixed amounts are tax excluded; tax-included prices (per tax or company-wide) are handled, and fixed amounts stay exact with both Round per Line and Round Globally.
Fiscal positions
Discount lines use the taxes already mapped on the lines; the expense account is mapped by the fiscal position.
Discount above the amount
Blocked with a clear message; no line can go below zero, and the preview shows the warning before you apply.
Already billed lines
Excluded from the wizard; billed discount lines cannot be removed. Posted bills, locked or cancelled orders and down-payment lines are refused.
Confirmed orders
Discounts can still change before billing; receipts validated afterwards use the new net price, and removed discount lines are set to quantity 0.
Quantity changes
Odoo 19 recomputes a line discount from the vendor pricelist when the quantity changes; discounts set by the wizard are kept. Other lines follow the standard logic.
Vendor pricelist on confirmation
Wizard discounts are not saved as vendor discounts, and the discount product is never added to a vendor pricelist with a negative price.
Units of measure and currencies
Quantity tiers use the product unit of measure; amount tiers convert the document currency into the company currency at the document date.
Multi-company
Setting, discount product, tiers and log are per company, with record rules. A company without the setting cannot open the wizard.

Security

Uses the standard Purchase and Invoicing groups; no new group to manage.
